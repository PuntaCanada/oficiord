Your safety comes first

OficioRD is a place to discover customer and professional listings. The platform does not provide listed services or participate in agreements, quotations, payments or the work performed between parties.

  • Check the other person's identity, references and experience before agreeing to any work.
  • Do not send advance payments to people you have not met and verified. Even after verification, agree in writing on the work, price, timing and payment terms.
  • Never share passwords, verification codes, banking details or sensitive documents with strangers.
  • Be wary of pressure to pay immediately or move conversations to suspicious channels. Contact the authorities if you face threats or suspected crimes.

Each person makes their own decisions and contracts and should use due care. OficioRD does not guarantee third parties' identities, work quality, licenses or financial reliability. This notice does not override consumer rights or any legal duties of the platform.

Support, complaints, suspected fraud and privacy requests: email soporte.oficiord@outlook.com. For general enquiries, use oficiord@outlook.com. Never include passwords, banking details or sensitive documents in your message.
