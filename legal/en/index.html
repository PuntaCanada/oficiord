Privacy policy under preparation

Once the service is activated, it is intended to process private email addresses and job or professional listing descriptions in Cloudflare D1; consent-based contact enquiries; reports and technical anti-abuse data through Cloudflare Turnstile. Authorized moderators can inspect and delete records in a private panel. Only approved listings, never their private contact email addresses, appear publicly.

Before accepting real user data, identify the actual controller and any processors, confirm the lawful basis and international data flows, document recipients and access from Germany, define real retention periods, deletion and rights-request procedures, and describe service providers and security measures. No automatic retention limit has yet been implemented; none is promised.

Free marketplace terms — draft

Browsing is free; the platform offers no accounts, payments or bookings. Any future listing must be genuine and posted by someone entitled to do so; all submissions require moderation and can be rejected or removed for safety. Do not publish bank details, access codes, private home addresses, third-party data without permission, fraudulent or discriminatory content. Service scope, price and payments are agreed directly by the parties; OficioRD does not perform the listed work. This does not waive mandatory legal duties or consumer rights.

Safety, moderation and deletion

Listings and reports will be reviewed before action. Enquiries first reach a private queue and may be relayed manually with the sender's reply email to the listing owner only after review and subject to applicable notice and consent. Reports may result in listing removal. Access and deletion requests go to the support address. Actual retention and processing rules must be finalized before launch.

Real submission and contact forms must remain disabled until operator details and legal review are complete.
